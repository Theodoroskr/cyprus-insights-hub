# Agent rules

- `directory_companies` is the master company record; curated `companies` rows link to it via `directory_company_id` and their old profile page redirects. Why: one company, one page, one URL.
- News↔company/person links, company events and regulatory records carry a `status`; only `approved`/`published` rows are public, and company links auto-approve only on a registration number, a `company_aliases` entry (curated, or former/trading names harvested from official registers), or a single unique `name_key` match (trailing city/branch words dropped; dissolved firms and unregistered placeholders don't count as rivals) — all through `_shared/companyMatcher.ts`; ambiguous names go to review. Why: no unsupported claims about people or firms.
- People are never auto-merged by name; name matches go to `match_review_queue`. Why: identical names are common and errors are reputational.
- Regulatory data is split by `record_type` (licensed, warning, enforcement); a warning must never be shown as a company's own sanction. Why: legal accuracy.
- Scrapers only collect from sources with `access_status = 'active'`. Why: no collection until reuse rights are confirmed.
- Entity extraction (`extract-entities`) has its own hourly cron at :30 (after the :00 scrape) and is not also triggered from `scrape-sources`. Why: linking must not depend on a scrape batch finishing, and one trigger avoids duplicate runs.
- Custom Studio uses the `custom-studio` banner placement and shows a labelled "Advertise here" placeholder (as do the other homepage slots) when no live, in-date image is available; sponsored article pages remain separate. Why: reuse banner scheduling without reserving empty homepage space.
- Homepage banner slots are a fixed list in `PLACEHOLDER_SLOTS` (`AdSlot.tsx`) mirrored in the Banners admin; each slot ID appears once per page, and hubs/articles use `hub-top`/`article-inline`. Why: prevent repeated ads and keep placements bookable.
- Homepage lead and feed share `useBlendedNews`: stable same-day source/topic mixing and deduplication happen before slicing; publication dates remain unchanged. Why: prevent scraping batches dominating the feed or repeating between sections.
- `cna_articles` are stored in English; Greek sources are translated at scrape time (and backfilled via `translate-articles`) with `original_language` recording the source language. Why: one-language editorial product.
- CySEC licences come only from the official register pages, parsed directly by `_shared/cysecRegister.ts` (no AI); a company's CySEC badge is always recomputed from all its `regulated_entities` CySEC rows, preferring a current licence. Why: real licence numbers/statuses, and one firm can hold old and new licences.
- Central Bank authorisations come only from the official CBC credit-institutions page and its EMI/PI `.xlsx` registers, parsed by `_shared/cbcRegister.ts` (no AI); a company's `cbc_*` badge is recomputed from all its CBC `regulated_entities` rows. Regulator imports fold unregistered placeholder profiles into the registered record via the service-only `merge_directory_placeholder`. Why: one firm, one profile, with real licence data.
- News duplicate checks use `canonicalArticleUrl` (keeps meaningful query strings like `?guid=`/`?id=`, drops tracking params), and a listing page that is a 404/“not found” screen or yields no articles is recorded as the source's `last_error`. Error-screen articles (`articlePageProblem`, `_shared/missingPage.ts`) are never stored or posted. Why: moved pages and file-handler links otherwise fail silently.
- Privileged backend functions (scrapers, importers, translators, AI generators, full digest runs) start with `_shared/requireTrustedCaller.ts`: scheduled jobs send `x-cron-secret` (value in service-only `internal_secrets`), otherwise the caller must be an admin. Why: service-role writes must never be reachable by anonymous visitors.
- Premium trade datasets (country/sector breakdowns, AI summaries) are readable only via `has_premium_access()` RLS; the page skips those queries for non-premium users. Why: the paywall must be enforced by the database, not by a blur.
- Resource Hub content lives in `resources.content` (Markdown) with `reviewed_at`; reports listed in `LIVE_REPORTS` render from live tables instead of stored text. Why: no stale or invented figures.
- Real Estate Intelligence (`/real-estate`) reads only `published` rows of `re_sale_contracts`/`re_transfers`, filled by `ingest-real-estate` from the data.gov.cy 1007/1005 CSVs (parsed in `_shared/realEstateParse.ts`); new months publish automatically, changed historical figures go to `re_revisions` for admin approval, and blank cells are never stored as zero. Why: official statistics must never be silently overwritten or invented.
- Who's Who is in private build: `WHOISWHO_PUBLIC` in `src/lib/featureFlags.ts` gates its routes (`WhoIsWhoRoute`, admins only) and people links via `useWhoIsWhoVisible()`; menu/footer links hidden for all (admins: dashboard link), and it is kept out of the sitemap until launch. Why: build the section before visitors see it.
- `open_data_sources` is the single registry of official datasets (module, links, licence, check status); importers update their own row, and `discover-open-data` only lists a pasted data.gov.cy dataset (disabled) — data is imported only by a purpose-built importer. Tourism arrivals come from CYSTAT PxWeb table 2021010E via `ingest-tourism` (`_shared/tourismParse.ts`) with the same publish/revision rules as Real Estate. Why: one auditable list of sources, no unverified data published.
- Daily `check-open-data-health` (`_shared/openDataHealth.ts`) notifies admins only when `alert_state` changes. Why: stale data is flagged once.
- `/economy`: CYSTAT `econ_indicators` (weekly `ingest-economy`, `ECON_SPECS`) + Eurostat `stat_*` tables (daily `ingest-eurostat`, `_shared/eurostat.ts`, config in `stat_datasets`); new periods publish, changed values go to revisions. Why: official figures only.
- Leads: `InfocreditCta` → `submit-enquiry` → admin-only `commercial_enquiries` + admin bell; `cta_events` is anonymous counts. Why: no public PII.
- The sitemap is static files on the site domain: `scripts/generate-sitemap.ts` (prebuild) writes `public/sitemap.xml` + `public/sitemaps/*.xml` from public tables using helpers in `src/lib/sitemap.ts`, and keeps the old files if the database is unreachable. Why: search engines only accept sitemap URLs on the site's own host; new content is added on each publish.
- RSS: prebuild `generate-rss.ts` writes `public/rss.xml` + `public/rss/<hub>.xml`. Why: feed URLs must be on the site domain.
- Facebook auto-posting: hourly `post-to-facebook` (07:00–21:00 Cyprus) posts new `cna_articles` to the page chosen in the admin-only `facebook_autopost_settings`, spread evenly up to its daily limit and logged once per article in `facebook_posts` (rules in `_shared/facebookPosting.ts`); the token lives only in the Facebook Pages connector (a Page token posts through the connector directly; a User token has its page token fetched each run), and each hourly run posts at most 3 as photo posts (story image → least-recently-used picture from the section library `SOCIAL_IMAGE_LIBRARY`, tracked via `facebook_posts.image_url` → hub image → link post). Why: no duplicate or flood posting, and no token stored in the database.
- Head tags: `RouteSEODefaults` (App.tsx) sets a self-referencing canonical/og:url via `canonicalUrl()` (`src/lib/seo.ts`); `SEOHead` overrides; `index.html` has no canonical and its fallback meta tags carry `data-rh="true"`. Why: one canonical/description per page.
- Hourly `ping-indexnow` sends new pages to Bing once (`indexnow_submissions`). Why: discovery between publishes.
- `/directory/cysec-licensed-firms` lists CySEC `regulated_entities`, never warnings. Why: licence data only.
- Postbuild `generate-story-previews.ts` writes per-story `dist/article/<id>/index.html` head tags. Why: preview robots skip JS.
