# Frontend rules

- Homepage blocks (lead, Latest, Full coverage, feed) split via `partitionHomepage` (`src/lib/homepageStories.ts`). Why: no story twice.
- `/rankings` uses only `get_rankings()` (security invoker) + `market_quotes`, each list sourced. Why: official data only.
- Top menu: `src/config/siteSections.ts`. Why: one list for menu and tests.
- Hub front pages share `HubMasthead` (own `wide-banner` top slot from `HUB_TOP_SLOTS`) + `HubLeadStories` (TourismHub reads the `tourism` vertical; fills the column under the 3 cards with `MoreStories` + own 728×90 slot from `HUB_MID_SLOTS`; homepage uses Most Read + `homepage-lead`); `HubLayout landing` drops the `hub-top` strip. Why: same structure, one banner per page.
- Google News: prebuild writes `public/news-sitemap.xml` (cna_articles first published ≤48h, via `recentNews`/`buildNewsSitemap` in `src/lib/sitemap.ts`); Deputy Ministry of Tourism releases enter by hand (`MinistryReleaseForm`, rules in `src/lib/ministryRelease.ts`) as drafts, deduped by canonical URL, near-matches tagged "Possible duplicate". Why: the gov.cy firewall blocks crawling; nothing publishes without an editor.
- Tourism news is its own `tourism` article vertical, set at collection time by `isTourismStory` (`supabase/functions/_shared/tourismNews.ts`, also used by the site) and shown at `/explore/news` (TourismHub "News" tab). Why: one section per story, filed once, not re-guessed on every page.
- Hand-written stories/press releases: Articles tab "New story / press release" (`NewStoryDialog`, rules in `src/lib/manualStory.ts`) inserts `cna_articles`; press releases always carry the "Press release" tag (shown as a badge on the story page) and name the issuer. The form picks an edition (Cyprus/English, Greece/Greek, or both): Greek text goes to `*_el` fields, Greece-only rows fill the required `title`/`summary` from the Greek; the story page reads Greece-only stories in Greek via `src/lib/storyLanguage.ts`. Why: readers must see what is paid/issued material, not reporting.
- EnergyHub (`/energy`, `/energy/news`, `EnergyPage`) reads the `energy` article vertical, filed at collection time by `isEnergyStory` (`supabase/functions/_shared/energyNews.ts`, after the tourism rule); topics are `ENERGY_TOPICS` matched on title/summary or tags, editors feature a story with the "EnergyHub featured" tag. Energy sources sit in `content_sources` (`target_vertical='energy'`, `auto_publish=false`) and stay inactive until each listing is validated. Why: one story record, filed once, nothing published without an editor.
- Editor highlights: `cna_articles.featured_until` (Articles tab "Highlight" menu); `useBlendedNews` puts in-date highlights first via `pinHighlighted` (`src/lib/highlight.ts`). Why: hold a story at the top for a set time without changing its publish date.
- Story pictures: publisher logos/default share images (`_shared/genericImage.ts`) and photos another story already uses are never stored as `image_url`; story lists choose pictures via `distinctNeighbours`/`listImage`. Why: nearby stories must not look identical.
- Story URLs are `/article/<id>/<headline-slug>` via `articlePath` (`src/lib/articleUrl.ts`); the id-only URL still loads and is rewritten, and postbuild previews are written at both. Why: readable links without breaking old shares.
- Banner views/clicks go through `src/lib/bannerTracking.ts` (`trackBanner` keepalive call to `track_banner`, `useBannerImpression`); own-page banner links navigate in-app after recording. Why: a page change must not cancel the count.

- Phone app shell: `public/manifest.webmanifest` (install only, no service worker; any + maskable icons; iOS launch screens in `public/splash/`, each linked from `index.html`) + `MobileTabBar` (md:hidden; Menu fires `bh:open-menu`, opened by TopNavigation). Why: installable without stale-cache risk; one menu drawer.
- Greece edition: `/greece` (`GreecePage`, config in `src/lib/edition.ts`) shows only `cna_articles` with `edition @> '{gr}'` (Greek text in `*_el`, `gr_section`, `organisation_slug`); one record can carry `{cy,gr}`; GR sources in `content_sources` (`country='GR'`, `ingestion_status`) stay inactive until their listing is verified; `scrape-sources` files GR-source stories via `grEditionFields` (`_shared/greeceEdition.ts`: edition `{gr}`, `gr_section` = source section, bank `organisation_slug`, banking filter tags, Greek title/summary); Cyprus lists, social posts, digests, RSS and Most read only take `edition @> '{cy}'`; `GR_DOMAIN_LIVE` switches Greece canonicals to businesshub.gr. Why: one story record, edition-scoped feeds, no unverified collection.
- Greece-related label: `cna_articles.greece_related` marks stories to present on BusinessHub.gr later, set at collection/creation by `isGreeceRelated` (`supabase/functions/_shared/greeceRelated.ts`: Greece-edition rows, or Greece/Greek banks/Athens named in headline, summary or tags — never the body) and toggled by editors in the Articles tab ("Greece-related" filter). It is a label only and never adds `gr` to `edition`. Why: collect the GR backlog without publishing unreviewed stories on the Greece edition.
